Questions

Why crypto only?

It keeps card data out of the platform and there is no payment processor to depend on. The trade-off is manual verification: a person checks the transaction before the licence is issued.

What happens after I submit the transaction?

You get an order code immediately. The owner matches the transfer to the amount and network, then emails the licence key to the address on the order. Order lookup shows the current state.

What does the licence unlock today?

A STIX 2.1 export of the public vulnerability and indicator feeds, plus the Pro console that shows entitlement status. Watchlists, correlated alerts, and webhooks are on the roadmap and are not charged for.

What if I send the wrong amount or the wrong network?

Wrong-network transfers cannot be recovered by us. An underpayment is visible in the review and can be topped up before approval; reply to the review email with the second transaction ID.

Where is my data kept?

Orders, subscriptions, and licence hashes live in a private Cloudflare D1 database, never in the public repository. The site stores no card data and hashes the IP address attached to an order.
