    What the layers are

    • Victim claims — ransomware leak-site victims published in the last 72 hours, shown in the country the claim names (ransomware.live).
    • Phishing hosting — hostnames in the current OpenPhish snapshot, resolved at collection time; the country is where the IP is registered, not where the attacker sits.
    • No source-to-destination arcs. No publishable feed records attacker origin, so the map shows where events are recorded rather than inventing a path.
    • IP geolocation: IP Geolocation by DB-IP, CC BY 4.0. Phishing hosts: OpenPhish.
