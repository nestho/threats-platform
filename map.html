    What the layers are

    • Victim claims — ransomware leak-site victims published in the last 72 hours, shown in the country the claim names (ransomware.live). These are claims, not independently verified incidents.
    • Phishing hosting — hostnames in the latest OpenPhish snapshot, resolved at collection time; the country is where the IP is registered, not where the attacker sits. This inventory is not replayed as a time series.
    • Honeypot SSH — connection attempts received by this project's Cowrie sensor, grouped by minute and source-IP country (DB-IP). The public feed contains no source IPs or credentials; geolocation is approximate and does not identify a person's location.
    • No source-to-destination arcs. The honeypot observes inbound connections only; the destination is this sensor, not a global victim network.
    • Public sensor feed: minute/country SSH aggregates; source IPs are not published.
    • IP geolocation: IP Geolocation by DB-IP, CC BY 4.0. Phishing hosts: OpenPhish.
